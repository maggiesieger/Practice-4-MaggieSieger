Serves 4 · 45 minutes

Slow BBQ Chicken

Low heat, a lid, and sauce added late so it never burns.

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken thighs, bone in
  • 1 tbs smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 cup barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Heat the oven
  2. Season the chicken
  3. Bake covered
  4. Sauce and finish
  5. Rest and serve
1

Heat the oven

Set it to 325 degrees. Low and slow is the whole trick.

Back to the steps

2

Season the Chicken

Mix the paprika, salt, pepper, and garlic power. Rub it in.

Back to the steps

3

Bake covered

Oil the pan, chicken skin side up, foil on tight. Forty minutes. Do not peek.

Sauce and finish.

Rest and serve
